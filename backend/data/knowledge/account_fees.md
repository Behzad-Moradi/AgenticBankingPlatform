# Account Fees

## Everyday Account

The Everyday Account has no monthly account-keeping fee.

ATM withdrawals made through the bank's own ATM network are free.

Withdrawals from third-party ATMs may incur a fee charged by the ATM operator.

## Savings Account

The Savings Account has no monthly account-keeping fee.

Customers can transfer funds between their own Everyday and Savings accounts without a transfer fee.
